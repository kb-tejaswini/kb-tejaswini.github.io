[6] Nov 2026 · Clickable UPI prototype · HTML, Figma

Receive Shield

Problem. UPI credits every payment automatically, so the person receiving money cannot refuse it. When a payment turns out to be linked to fraud, innocent shopkeepers, freelancers and sellers can have their bank accounts frozen.

What I built. A concept for a new UPI setting that lets receivers accept or return a payment from an unknown payer before it lands in their account. The prototype has 11 clickable screens, from onboarding and risk checks to held-payment review and account-hold help, with a matching Figma design. A reserve-then-credit flow like this would need NPCI approval.

[5] Nov 2026 · Clickable EV prototype · HTML

WearWallet

Problem. EV owners can't see how their charging and driving habits wear down the battery, the most expensive part of the car, or what that wear means for their warranty and resale value.

What I built. A pay-as-you-wear EV battery warranty and app. It shows what every charge, trip and parked hour costs the battery in rupees, rewards careful use with Health Credits, charges a small top-up for heavy use without ever voiding the warranty, and produces a verified Wear Statement for resale.

[4] Nov 2026 · Pricing engine prototype · Python, REST API, Streamlit

Spice and Bun Co. Pricing

Problem. What price will deliver better business results? A restaurant chain needs to know, for every store and menu item, how customers respond to price changes, discounts and competitor moves.

What I built. A pricing engine that measures price sensitivity for each store and item, simulates the effect of any price, discount or competitor move on orders, revenue and contribution, and recommends a price within business guardrails, with an uncertainty range and sensitivity analysis. It includes 20 data-quality checks, cross-validation of 4 models, a REST API, a dashboard and an insights report.
